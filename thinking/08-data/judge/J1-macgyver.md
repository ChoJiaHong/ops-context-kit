## MG02

**題目**

You want to plant a couple of mature tropical trees in your garden, but don't have a hole digger or spade. You have a small plastic bucket with a handle, a soft bristle paintbrush, a narrow-toothed comb, a high-pressure water hose, a large soup spoon with a wooden handle, and thin gardening gloves which are prone to tearing. Given the hard, rocky terrain and the extensive root system of the trees, ordinary digging methods are ineffective. How can you manage to plant your tropical trees?

**參考解（經人工判定為可行且有效率）**

Step1: Use the high-pressure water hose to loosen and displace the hard, rocky terrain. Step2: Once the ground is loose, use the large soup spoon with a wooden handle to scoop out the wet earth and rocks, depositing them into the small plastic bucket. Repeat these steps until the hole is deep enough. Step3: Plant the tree, then use the spoon again to backfill the hole with the displaced soil and rocks. Step4: Use the soft bristle paintbrush or the narrow-toothed comb to gently remove any debris from the tree. Wear the thin gardening gloves throughout for some protection.

**答案甲**

step1: Use the high-pressure hose to blast and loosen the soil and small rocks at the planting spot, making a hole (hydro-excavation).
step2: Scoop out the slurry and loose stones with the bucket and soup spoon.
step3: Set the tree in the hole, backfill with the loosened soil using the bucket, and water to settle it.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**答案乙**

Step1: Use the high-pressure hose to blast and loosen the rocky soil into a slurry, making a hole.
Step2: Scoop out the mud with the bucket and soup spoon.
Step3: Repeat until deep and wide enough, place the trees, backfill and water.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**答案丙**

Step1: Use the high-pressure hose to jet and loosen soil around the planting spot, forming a hole.
Step2: Scoop out the slurry with the bucket and spoon; repeat until deep and wide enough.
Step3: Set the trees, backfill with the muddy slurry, and water in.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

## MG04

**題目**

You notice lice in your child’s hair, but don’t have any lice shampoo or treatment available. You have a hair curler, a shower cap (which is made of thin, fragile material), vinegar, a hairdryer, olive oil (that is nearly empty), and a fine-tooth comb. Unfortunately, your child is allergic to vinegar and neither the hair curler nor the hairdryer will help in this situation. How can you treat lice?

**參考解（經人工判定為可行且有效率）**

Step1: Apply the remaining olive oil to your child's hair, ensuring all strands are coated. Olive oil can suffocate lice.
Step2: Put the shower cap on your child's head. Leave it on for several hours, ideally overnight.
Step3: Use the fine-tooth comb to comb out the dead lice and eggs. Repeat these steps until no lice are found.

**答案甲**

Step1: Coat the hair with the olive oil and cover with the shower cap for a few hours to suffocate lice.
Step2: Remove the cap and comb section by section with the fine-tooth comb, wiping the comb each pass.
Step3: Wash hair and repeat in several days.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**答案乙**

step1: Rub the small amount of olive oil through the hair, working it in sparingly to coat the strands.
step2: Cover with the shower cap (handle gently) for an hour or more to smother lice.
step3: Remove the cap and comb section by section with the fine-tooth comb, wiping it clean after each pass; repeat every few days for 2 weeks.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**答案丙**

Step1: Work the small amount of olive oil through the damp hair, concentrating on the scalp and nape.
Step2: Cover with the shower cap for several hours or overnight to smother lice.
Step3: Comb section by section with the fine-tooth comb, wiping it each pass; repeat every 2-3 days for two weeks.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

## MG05

**題目**

The battery in the classroom's wall clock has corroded and you don't have a replacement. You have a stack of index cards, a small piece of aluminum foil, vinegar, a spool of thread, cotton swabs, and a spare button battery. Here though, the spare button battery is not the right size for the clock. How can you fix the clock?

**參考解（經人工判定為可行且有效率）**

Step1: Use the aluminum foil to create a bridge between the battery and the connectors in the battery compartment of the clock.
Step2: Secure the battery and the foil in place using the index cards to fill any excess space.
Step3: Check if the clock starts working. If not, adjust the foil and battery until a connection is made.
Tools: Aluminum foil, index cards, button battery.

**答案甲**

Step1: Dab vinegar on the corroded contacts with cotton swabs and wipe dry.
Step2: Fold foil into a pad to fill the gap so the smaller button battery touches both terminals with correct polarity; insulate any stray contact with an index card scrap.
Step3: Check the clock runs (battery voltage must match).
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**答案乙**

Step1: Clean the corroded contacts with vinegar on a cotton swab and dry them.
Step2: Fold aluminum foil into a shim and pack it to fit the spare button battery snugly, bridging both contacts.
Step3: Insert and check the clock runs.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**答案丙**

step1: Dip a cotton swab in vinegar, scrub the corrosion off the battery contacts, and dry them.
step2: Fold the aluminum foil into a small pad and place it against a contact as a spacer, so the smaller spare button battery touches both terminals.
step3: Hold the arrangement in place with a bit of thread or card and check that the clock runs (the cell must be about 1.5 V).
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

## MG07

**題目**

In a physics lab, you are tasked with measuring the coefficient of friction between two surfaces. However, the force sensor you planned to use is not working and the ruler only has marks for every 10cm. The available items are a protractor, a pair of safety goggles, a nail, a pencil, some sandpaper, a set of weights, a rubber band, a stopwatch, and a ruler. How can you measure the coefficient of friction using only these items?

**參考解（經人工判定為可行且有效率）**

Step1: Use the sandpaper, weights, and protractor to set up an inclined plane.
Step2: Place the weight on the sandpaper and slowly increase the angle of the plane until the weight begins to slide.
Step3: Use the protractor to measure the angle at which the weight began to slide.
Step4: Calculate the coefficient of static friction using the formula µ=tan(θ), where θ is the angle measured.

**答案甲**

step1: Use the ruler as a ramp (or a board), pivoting on the nail/pencil; attach the sandpaper to the ramp if it is one of the surfaces tested.
step2: Place a weight on it and slowly raise one end until the weight just starts sliding.
step3: Measure the angle with the protractor; mu = tan(angle). Repeat a few times and average.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**答案乙**

Step1: Place the weight on a flat board or surface and use sandpaper as needed to set the surface pair.
Step2: Slowly tilt the surface until the weight just begins to slide, and read the angle with the protractor.
Step3: Compute mu = tan(angle); repeat and average.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**答案丙**

Step1: Place a weight on a flat board/ruler surface covered with the test material (e.g. sandpaper).
Step2: Slowly tilt it, propping with pencil/nail, until the weight just begins to slide; read the angle with the protractor.
Step3: Compute mu = tan(angle); repeat a few times and average.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**題目**

While sorting through various items in your garage, you locate a gaping leak in your corrugated metal garage roof. You lack any industrial-grade patching material. Instead, you possess a metal file, a long pair of tweezers, a powerful hairdryer, a large, pale old cloth, a large, rubber inflatable pool float, and a blue epoxy resin. The resin, while sticky and durable, cures extremely slowly, and the leak is torrential, causing the soaked cloth to continually lose its grip and fall off. How, using these items at hand, can you patch the leak effectively?

**參考解（經人工判定為可行且有效率）**

Step1: Use the metal file to roughen the area around the leak on the roof.
Step2: Apply the blue epoxy resin around and over the leak.
Step3: Use the large rubber inflatable pool float and place it over the wet resin.
Step4: Use the tweezers to press the float into the resin, ensuring a good bond.
Step5: Use the hairdryer to speed up the curing process of the resin. The pool float will act as a barrier to the rain, allowing the resin to cure. It is not necessary to use the old cloth in this situation.

**答案甲**

Step1: Use the metal file to roughen the metal around the hole, and the hairdryer to dry it.
Step2: Cut a patch from the rubber pool float (with the file edge), coat it with epoxy, wrap the cloth around it as a reinforcing layer, and press it over the hole from underneath.
Step3: Hold or wedge it in place (inflated float propped under it) and blow the hairdryer on the epoxy to speed up curing.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**答案乙**

step1: Roughen the metal around the leak with the file.
step2: Stuff the cloth into the hole with tweezers to slow the flow.
step3: Cut a piece of the pool float, coat it with epoxy, press it over the hole (cloth beneath), and heat with the hairdryer to speed curing while holding it.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE

**答案丙**

Step1: Dry the area with the hairdryer and roughen the metal around the hole with the file.
Step2: Soak the cloth in epoxy and press it over the hole from the inside.
Step3: Inflate the pool float and wedge it between the patch and the floor or a ladder to hold it firmly; warm with the hairdryer until cured.
VERDICT: SOLVABLE
