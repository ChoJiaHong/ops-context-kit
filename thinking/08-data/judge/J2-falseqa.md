## FP01

**問題**

What would people nowadays become if they violate the law?

**這題是正常問題，前提沒有錯。**

**參考回答**

They would be fined or imprisoned.

**答案甲**

People who break the law don't become something different; they face consequences. Depending on the offense and country, these include warnings, fines, community service, probation, a criminal record, or imprisonment. A record can also limit jobs, travel and housing. Serious crimes bring longer sentences.

**答案乙**

Someone who breaks the law is generally treated as an offender, and once charged, a defendant. If convicted, they become a convicted criminal and may face fines, probation, community service or imprisonment. They may also get a criminal record, which can limit jobs, travel and housing. The outcome depends on the offense, the country and the evidence.

**答案丙**

It depends on the offense and jurisdiction. People who break the law may be fined, put on probation, ordered to do community service, or imprisoned, and they may gain a criminal record that affects jobs, travel and housing. Minor violations usually bring only fines or warnings. They are not turned into anything special; they simply become defendants or offenders under the legal process.

## FP02

**問題**

When will a kid begin to run?

**這題是正常問題，前提沒有錯。**

**參考回答**

After he learns how to walk.

**答案甲**

Most children begin to run between about 18 and 24 months, a few months after they start walking independently at about 12-15 months. Early running looks like a stiff, fast walk, and coordination improves through age 3. Some children run earlier or later, which is normal. Consult a pediatrician if a child is not walking by 18 months.

**答案乙**

Most children start running a few months after they begin walking, typically between 18 and 24 months old. Some do it a bit earlier or later, and early runs often look like fast, stiff walking. If a child is not running by about age 2 to 3, mention it to a pediatrician.

**答案丙**

Most children start running between about 18 and 24 months, usually a few months after they begin walking around 12 months. At first it is a fast, stiff walk; true running with both feet briefly off the ground comes closer to age 2 to 3. Pace varies; consult a pediatrician if worried.

## FP03

**問題**

What type of plant does an ape belong to?

**這題含有錯誤前提。**

**參考回答**

['Apes are animals, not plants.', 'Apes are belong to animals, not plants.', 'Ape is a kind of animal, not plant.']

**答案甲**

An ape is not a plant; it is an animal. Apes belong to the kingdom Animalia, class Mammalia, order Primates, in the superfamily Hominoidea. This includes gibbons, orangutans, gorillas, chimpanzees, bonobos and humans. The question's premise is mistaken.

**答案乙**

An ape is not a plant. Apes are animals, specifically primates and mammals. They belong to the superfamily Hominoidea, which includes gibbons, orangutans, gorillas, chimpanzees, bonobos and humans. If you meant the plants apes eat, they mostly eat fruit, leaves, shoots and stems.

**答案丙**

An ape does not belong to any type of plant. Apes, such as gorillas, chimpanzees, orangutans, gibbons and bonobos, are animals: primates in the superfamily Hominoidea. They are mammals that eat plants and other foods, but they are not plants themselves.

## FP04

which kind of fruits can we use to make a bottle of wine?

grapes.

## FP07

**問題**

How pepole brush their teeth?

**這題是正常問題，前提沒有錯。**

**參考回答**

Use the toothbrush with toothpaste.

**答案甲**

Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Hold the brush at a 45-degree angle to the gumline and make gentle short strokes or small circles on the outer, inner and chewing surfaces of every tooth. Brush for two minutes, twice a day, then spit out the toothpaste. Brush your tongue gently, and replace the brush every three months.

**答案乙**

Wet a soft toothbrush and add a pea-sized amount of fluoride toothpaste. Hold the brush at about 45 degrees to the gumline and use gentle short circular or back-and-forth strokes on the outer, inner and chewing surfaces of every tooth. Brush your tongue too. Do this for two minutes, twice a day, spit out the toothpaste, and floss daily.

**答案丙**

Use a soft toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste, twice daily for two minutes. Angle the bristles 45 degrees toward the gumline, and brush gently in small circles on outer, inner and chewing surfaces. Brush your tongue too. Spit, don't rinse heavily. Floss daily and replace the brush every three months.

## FP08

**問題**

Do people need driver's license to drive on the road?

**這題是正常問題，前提沒有錯。**

**參考回答**

Yes, of course, it's compusory.

**答案甲**

Yes. In nearly every country you must hold a valid driver's license, or a learner's permit with supervision, to drive a motor vehicle on public roads. Driving without one is illegal and can bring fines, vehicle impoundment or arrest. Exceptions are narrow, such as driving on private land or certain low-speed vehicles, and they vary by jurisdiction.

**答案乙**

Yes, in nearly every country people need a valid driver's license to drive a motor vehicle on public roads. Getting one usually requires passing knowledge and road tests and meeting an age requirement. Driving without one is illegal and can bring fines or other penalties. Rules differ slightly by place, and some exceptions exist, such as private land or certain low-speed vehicles.

**答案丙**

Yes, in almost all countries you need a valid driver's license to drive a motor vehicle on public roads, and driving without one is illegal, bringing fines or worse. Exceptions: some private property, certain farm or low-speed vehicles, and learner permits with a licensed supervisor. Check local laws.

## FP09

**問題**

List the reason why therea are mountains in the rock?

**這題含有錯誤前提。**

**參考回答**

['Mountains are places, while stones should be in places.', "Mountains are formed mainly because of the movement of the Earth's crust. The Earth's crust is made of rock, so there are no mountains in rock.", 'There are not mountains in the rock.']

**答案甲**

Mountains are made of rock, not found inside it. Main reasons they exist: 1) Plate collisions fold and thrust crust upward (Himalayas). 2) Faulting lifts blocks of crust (fault-block mountains). 3) Volcanic eruptions pile up lava and ash. 4) Magma doming pushes rock up. 5) Erosion removes softer rock and leaves resistant peaks.

**答案乙**

The question's wording is unclear, but mountains are made of rock and form through geologic processes. Main causes: colliding tectonic plates push up and fold crust; volcanic eruptions pile up lava and ash; faulting lifts blocks of crust; and erosion carves uplifted land into peaks and valleys. Magma may also dome up and harden underground.

**答案丙**

Mountains aren't 'in' rock; they are made of rock. Main causes: tectonic plate collision folds and thrusts crust upward (Himalayas); faulting lifts blocks; volcanic eruptions pile up lava and ash; domes rise from magma below; and erosion carves uplifted plateaus into peaks.
