# Arm B: bidirectional loop (English rendering of thinking/double-loop/bidirectional-loop/SKILL.md)

Use this method for each problem:

Move back and forth between two opposite directions.

There must be exactly two directions, and they must be opposite: the end point of one direction is the starting point of the other, so you can keep moving forward.

What the two directions are, when to switch, when to stop, what counts as progress, and what to produce are all up to you, depending on the situation.
